## What is SOL?
SOL na token for Solana blockchain. Faster and cheaper than bank.

- Speed: 65,000 transactions per second
- Cost: $0.001 per transaction vs bank $20
- Use: For payments and USDC stablecoin

## How SME for PH Fit Use Am
1. Pay supplier with USDC on Solana - e dey reach in 2 seconds
2. Save 90% for transfer fees
3. All payments dey recorded for blockchain - no story

## Risk for SME
- SOL price dey change - use USDC stablecoin instead
- Use licensed apps like Busha or Quidax to change to Naira
- Always test with $1 first
